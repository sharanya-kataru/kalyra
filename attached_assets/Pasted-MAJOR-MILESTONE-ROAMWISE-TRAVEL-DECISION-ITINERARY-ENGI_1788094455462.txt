============================================================
PRODUCT NORTH STAR
============================================================

Roamwise is NOT simply an AI itinerary generator.

Roamwise is a TRAVEL DECISION ENGINE.

The core question is:

"Given who this traveler is, their available time, budget, interests, travel style, and constraints, what combination of destinations, experiences, transportation, time allocation, and spending creates the maximum Experience Value?"

The product should feel like:

"A knowledgeable travel expert understands me, challenges weak decisions, explains the tradeoffs, and helps me create the best possible version of my trip."

Every major recommendation should help the traveler make a decision.

Do not prioritize generating more travel content simply for the sake of having more content.

1. FIX DATE INPUT — REQUIRED
============================================================

The current application is too restrictive with travel dates.

It currently expects input similar to:

2026-09-18 to 2026-09-29

This MUST be fixed.

Users should NOT need to know a specific date syntax.

Prefer the following UX:

START DATE
[ date picker / typed date ]

END DATE
[ date picker / typed date ]

The user should be able to select dates visually OR type common human-readable formats.

Support reasonable formats including:

September 18, 2026
September 18th, 2026
Sep 18 2026
9/18/2026
09/18/2026
9/18/26
2026-09-18

If retaining a single range field anywhere, also understand:

September 18-29, 2026
September 18 to September 29, 2026
Sep 18 - Sep 29, 2026
9/18/2026 - 9/29/2026
9/18/26 to 9/29/26
2026-09-18 to 2026-09-29

Internally normalize dates to:

YYYY-MM-DD

before storing them or sending them to planning logic.

IMPORTANT:

Do NOT use OpenAI to calculate dates.

Application code must calculate:

start_date
end_date
total_days
total_nights

Validate:

- start date exists
- end date exists
- end date occurs after start date
- itinerary duration matches calculated trip duration

Example:

September 18 through September 29

must never produce:

0 nights

If dates cannot be parsed, show a friendly frontend error such as:

"We couldn't understand those dates. Try selecting them from the calendar or entering a date like September 18, 2026."

Do not expose parsing exceptions.

2. KEEP ANALYSIS SEPARATE FROM ITINERARY GENERATION
============================================================

The intended Roamwise product flow is:

Questionnaire
↓
Travel Decision Analysis
↓
Roamwise Recommendation
↓
User reviews strategy/tradeoffs
↓
Generate My Trip
↓
Complete Trip Dashboard
↓
Continuous Refinement

DO NOT collapse analysis and itinerary generation into one step.

The analysis step is a core product differentiator.

Before generating a complete itinerary, Roamwise should answer:

- Which destinations best fit this traveler?
- Is the user's proposed trip realistic?
- Is their destination allocation sensible?
- Should some destinations receive more or less time?
- Should anything be removed?
- Is another destination worth considering?
- Is the route geographically sensible?
- Is the trip too rushed?
- Where is transportation consuming too much vacation time?
- Where will the traveler's budget create the most Experience Value?
- What tradeoffs exist?

The user should remain in control.

Roamwise may challenge the user's choices but must allow them to continue with their original preference.

3. OPPORTUNITY COST IS THE CORE DECISION PRINCIPLE
============================================================

Every travel decision consumes scarce resources:

- vacation days
- money
- energy
- transportation time
- attention

Therefore Roamwise should NOT simply ask:

"Is Milan a good destination?"

It should ask:

"For THIS traveler, is spending another vacation day in Milan better than spending that day somewhere else?"

Do not simply ask:

"Is this activity worth $150?"

Ask:

"Does spending $150 on this experience create more Experience Value than alternative uses of that money?"

This opportunity-cost framework should influence:

- destination ranking
- number of nights
- route planning
- activity selection
- transportation decisions
- budget recommendations
- accommodation recommendations
- itinerary modifications

7. PROACTIVE TRAVEL EXPERT BEHAVIOR
============================================================

Roamwise should respectfully challenge weak decisions.

Example:

Traveler wants:

4 nights Milan
2 nights Zermatt

Traveler strongly prefers:

mountains
nature
photography

Roamwise should be capable of explaining:

"Milan offers strong food, architecture, and urban experiences, but four nights may use more of your limited vacation time than necessary given your priorities. Reducing Milan and allocating additional time to Zermatt could improve your overall Experience Match."

The AI should follow this pattern:

1. ACKNOWLEDGE
"I understand you want four nights in Milan."

2. EXPLAIN TRADEOFF
"That leaves less time for destinations that more strongly match your mountain and photography interests."

3. RECOMMEND
"I recommend reducing Milan and allocating additional time to Zermatt."

Never behave as though the traveler is wrong.

Explain the opportunity cost and let the traveler choose.
