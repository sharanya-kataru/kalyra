PROJECT FIX: Make the generated trip fully functional end-to-end.

The current app still has incomplete trip-generation behavior.

Observed problems:
- Budget input/output is using euros. The product should use USD by default.
- Generated trips show 0 nights even when dates are provided.
- Average destination cost sometimes displays ~$NaN/day.
- The route may show only a single broad country instead of usable destinations/cities.
- The Day By Day tab does not yet produce a complete itinerary.
- Destination cards are incomplete.
- Budget breakdown appears to be estimated without enough real trip data.
- The trip looks visually complete but is not functionally complete.

Do not redesign the UI. Keep the current look and layout.

Your task is to make the existing app function correctly.
