---
name: Roamwise product direction
description: Durable product principles for the personalized travel optimization experience.
---

Roamwise should optimize experience value rather than simply cheapest, fastest, or most popular options. Recommendations must explain tradeoffs in terms of traveler fit, uniqueness, limited vacation time, budget value, and transportation efficiency.

**Why:** The product differentiator is respectful challenge and transparent reasoning; a generic attraction list would undermine the core promise.

**How to apply:** Keep questionnaire inputs, AI prompts, scoring, and dashboard explanations connected to the traveler's stated preferences and constraints.
